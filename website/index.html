// THE PART EVERYONE IGNORES

Skins are taxable assets

HMRC, the IRS and most EU tax authorities treat gains on virtual items as taxable — in many places even when you never cash out of Steam Wallet. Marketplaces can report your gross proceeds; your per-item cost-basis records are your defence. Guessing at year end, or hoping a spreadsheet holds up, is how five-figure portfolios turn into problems.

01 · RECORDEvery buy is a lot

Each purchase is stored as a dated lot — quantity, unit cost, currency, and the exchange rate on that exact day. Sell years later and the acquisition side of the calculation is already done.

02 · MATCHThe right cost basis

Disposals are matched to lots using your jurisdiction's actual rules — UK Section 104 pooling with same-day and 30-day matching, FIFO, average cost, or specific identification.

03 · REPORTFile with confidence

A per-jurisdiction capital-gains report with the full disposal schedule, holding periods, allowance usage and estimated tax — in your tax authority's currency, ready for your accountant.
