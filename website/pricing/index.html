// QUESTIONS

The fine print, plainly

How do I buy?

Download the app, start the free trial, and upgrade from Settings → Vault Pro. Checkout and billing are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record — they handle VAT/sales tax and card security. You'll receive a licence key by email to activate the app.

What happens when I cancel?

Pro features stay unlocked until the end of the period you paid for, then the app drops back to the free tier. Your data is never deleted or held hostage — everything stays on your machine.

Do I need to be online?

Only for price refreshes and an occasional licence check. Pro stays unlocked for a 14-day grace period if the app can't reach the network, so a flaky connection never locks you out.

Refunds?

See our refund policy. Because there's a full 14-day trial with no card, you can verify everything works for your portfolio before paying a penny.

Estimates, not tax advice. Vault Pro produces estimates to help you organise your records. Always confirm your position with a qualified accountant before filing.
